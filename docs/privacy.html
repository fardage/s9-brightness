Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

S9 Brightness collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it never sends anything about you or your Mac to the developer.

What stays on your Mac

To control your monitor, the app keeps a few things locally, inside its own sandboxed storage on your Mac:

  • The monitor’s IP address, name, serial number and certificate fingerprint, so it can find the monitor again and make sure it’s talking to the same one.
  • The access token the monitor issues when you choose Allow, stored in the macOS keychain.
  • Your four brightness presets.

None of this leaves your Mac except as described below. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Your local network

The app searches your local network for Samsung monitors and sends brightness commands to the one you connect. This traffic goes directly between your Mac and your monitor and never passes through the internet or any server. macOS asks for your permission before the app can use the local network.

Update check (download version only)

The version downloaded from GitHub checks once a day whether a newer release exists by requesting the latest release from GitHub’s public API. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub, under GitHub’s privacy statement. No other information is sent. The Mac App Store version never makes this request; the App Store handles its updates.

Apple

If you install from the Mac App Store, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers when you have agreed to share them with app developers in macOS settings. These are anonymous and governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
